**Name** : Survivor: End of the World

**Short description** : Offline survival guide: first aid, water, fire, Morse, maps and GPS.

**Full description** :
When the Internet, the mobile network or the power goes down, Survivor: End of the World keeps working. Every guide and every tool works without a connection. Free, ad-free, no account needed.

🆘 LIFE-SAVING ACTIONS
• CPR, severe bleeding, choking (adult and infant), recovery position
• Burns, fractures, stroke, severe allergic reaction, asthma attack, heatstroke, hypothermia, bites
• Short numbered steps, dangers highlighted in red, sources given (WHO, European Resuscitation Council, Red Cross…)

📚 73 GUIDES IN 12 TOPICS
Emergencies and safety, first aid, water, fire and warmth, shelter, food, navigation, communicating without a network, energy, hygiene, preparation and emergency kit, knots.
• Make water safe to drink (boiling, bleach, sunlight), build a filter
• Light a fire, keep warm without electricity, avoid carbon monoxide
• Find north without a compass, using the sun, a watch or the stars
• 72-hour emergency kit, first-aid kit, two-week food stock: checklists you can tick

🗺️ OFFLINE MAPS AND GPS
• Download the map of your country or region once (OpenStreetMap), then use it with no network at all
• Your GPS position on the map, because GPS works without the Internet
• Your exact coordinates, ready to read out to rescuers

🔦 BUILT-IN TOOLS
• Flashlight, automatic SOS signal and strobe
• Morse: send (flash, sound, screen), listen through the microphone and translate, or translate a written code
• Compass
• CPR metronome at the right rhythm (110 per minute), with 30:2 mode

🌙 DESIGNED FOR HARD TIMES
• Night red mode: preserves night vision and stays discreet
• Large text, light theme for bright sunlight, dark theme that saves battery
• Fast search; direct access to life-saving actions; favorites
• In English and French

🔒 PRIVACY
No data collected, no ads. Microphone and location are optional and only used on the phone; nothing is recorded or sent. The Internet is only used to download maps.

⚠️ This app is not a substitute for first-aid training or for emergency services. In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 (911 in North America).
